How to read these numbers

Short definitions of every figure on this page.

BilledTotal charges sent out for the claims in the selection, at the clinic's full fee schedule, before any insurer discount.
CollectedMoney actually received on those claims from insurers and patients combined.
Contractual adjustmentsThe part of the fee that insurers never pay because the contract rate is lower than the fee schedule. Not a loss, it was never collectible.
Write-offsAmounts the clinic chose to give up: denials not appealed, courtesy discounts, small balances, timely-filing losses. This is real money lost.
Collection rateCollected ÷ (Billed − Contractual adjustments). The share of what was realistically collectible that has actually come in.
Balance still dueSum of the open balances on claims that still owe money. The amount still being worked.
Net change in A/RBilled − Collected − Contractual − Write-offs. How much the receivables balance grew over the period, net of credits.
Open balance by ageBalance still due, grouped by how long ago the service happened. The older the bucket, the harder it is to collect.
Outstanding (A/R)The official total of every unpaid balance in eCW on the report date, aged by claim date. The number to quote as "what we are owed".
Claim status codeThe workflow state eCW assigned to the claim (submitted, paid, denied, patient balance, etc.). Meaning of each code to be confirmed with billing.
